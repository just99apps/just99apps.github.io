Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 27, 2026

At 99Apps, your privacy is our priority. We believe that great applications should respect users' privacy. Our goal is to build useful applications without collecting or sharing unnecessary personal information.

1. Information Collection

We do not collect, store, or share users' personal information through our applications.

Our applications are designed with privacy in mind, and we aim to minimize data collection. Any information you enter or create within an application remains on your device unless the app clearly specifies otherwise.

2. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share users' personal information with third parties. We do not use our applications to track users or build personal profiles.

3. Permissions

Some applications may request specific Android permissions to provide their intended functionality. These permissions are used only for the relevant app features.

You can review or manage permissions at any time through your Android device settings.

4. Data Security

Since we prioritize local processing and minimal data collection, we aim to keep your information under your control.

5. Third-Party Services

Our applications are designed to minimize reliance on third-party services that collect personal information. If any application uses a third-party service in the future, its privacy practices will be disclosed in the relevant application or an updated privacy policy.

6. Children's Privacy

Our applications do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when necessary to reflect changes in our applications or services. Any updates will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
